# Headroom — Vault

living somewhere disconnected. It doesn't duplicate the build history —
`CLAUDE.md` stays the single source of truth for that (every phase, every bug
found and fixed, dated). This vault is for the things a dated changelog isn't
good at: decisions with their *why* pulled out on their own page, and real
findings that don't belong to any one commit.
